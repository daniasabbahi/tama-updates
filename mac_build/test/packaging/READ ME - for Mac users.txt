TAMA for Mac - Teaching, Assessment, Measuring Achievement
===========================================================

Which file do I need?
  Apple menu () > About This Mac. If "Chip" says Apple M1, M2, M3, M4 ... use
  "TAMA-Mac-Apple-silicon-....dmg". If it says "Processor ... Intel", use "TAMA-Mac-Intel-....dmg".
  Not sure? Use the Intel one: it runs on every Mac (a newer Mac may first ask to install
  "Rosetta"; click Install), and TAMA switches itself to the faster version with its first update.

Installing (only once)
  1. Double-click the .dmg file. A window opens with TAMA and the Applications folder.
  2. Drag TAMA onto the Applications folder. Then close the window and eject "TAMA"
     (the drive on the desktop or in the Finder sidebar).
  3. Open TAMA from Applications (or Launchpad).

The first time you open TAMA, the Mac warns you
  TAMA is not bought from the App Store and not signed with a paid Apple certificate, so the Mac
  shows a message like "Apple could not verify TAMA is free of malware" or "TAMA cannot be opened".
  This is normal for small apps. Do this once:
    a. Click "Done" (or "OK"). Do NOT click "Move to Trash".
    b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security. You will see
       "TAMA was blocked to protect your Mac". Click "Open Anyway", then type your Mac password.
    c. Click "Open Anyway" once more. From now on TAMA opens normally.
  On an older Mac (macOS 14 Sonoma or earlier) you can instead right-click TAMA in Applications,
  choose Open, and click Open.

  If nothing helps: open the Terminal app, paste this line and press Return, then open TAMA again:
      xattr -cr /Applications/TAMA.app

Using TAMA
  TAMA opens in its own window. The first start can take up to a minute. Close the window
  (or press Cmd+Q) to close TAMA.

First time: licence key
  TAMA shows a "computer code" and a short form. Send your request to Dania; she sends back a
  licence key by e-mail. Paste the key into TAMA and click Activate.
  To enter a new key later: menu "Licence" > "Enter or replace licence key".

Your data
  Everything you enter is saved on this Mac in the folder
      ~/Library/Application Support/TAMA
  (in the Finder: menu Go > Go to Folder..., paste the line above, press Return). Updates never
  change your data. To keep a backup, use "Download all my data" in TAMA, or copy the file
  app_data.json from that folder.

Updates
  You do not need to do anything, and you never need to install TAMA again. When Dania publishes
  a new version, TAMA downloads it quietly in the background while you work (the window title
  shows "Update ... is ready"). The next time you close TAMA and open it again, the new version
  opens. Your data and your licence key stay exactly as they are.
  This needs internet; without internet TAMA simply works with the version you have.

TAMA works without internet.
