TAMA online (test)

TAMA runs inside your web browser. Your courses and marks stay on this computer and are not sent to any server.

Your data is kept: inside this browser on this computer
This browser keeps your data inside the browser on this computer. (Chrome or Edge can also keep it in a folder you choose.) For a spare copy, use “Download all my data” on TAMA's Settings page.

The first time takes about a minute while TAMA loads into the browser. Works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on Windows and Mac.
